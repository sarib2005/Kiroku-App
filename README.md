# Kiroku-App

An anime, manga, manhwa, manhua and light novel tracker for Android. Built with React Native, Expo and Firebase.

> *Kiroku* (記録) means "record" in Japanese. Every episode, every chapter, on record.

Kiroku is one place to track everything you watch and read, so you never lose your place and never have to juggle five different lists.

Website: [kiroku-app.vercel.app](https://kiroku-app.vercel.app)

## Download

Get the latest APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/sarib2005/Kiroku-App/releases) page.

1. Download the `.apk` file from the latest release.
2. Open it on your Android phone and allow installation from your browser or file manager if asked.
3. Install and open Kiroku.

The app checks for updates on its own and lets you know when a new version is ready.

## Features

**Track everything**
- Anime, manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels, all in one app
- One Discover and one Library for anime and manga, with a simple switch at the top
- Statuses for Watching/Reading, Wishlist, On Hold, Dropped and Completed
- Episode, chapter and volume progress, so you always know where you left off
- "Mark as caught up" for anime that are still airing
- A library you can search, filter and group by franchise
- Hide individual titles from your public profile
- Can't find a title? Add it yourself, and once it has been checked it shows up for everyone to track, rate and comment on

**Build a habit**
- An activity heatmap on your profile that shows every day you watched or read
- Daily streaks with an animated flame that grows the longer you keep going
- Friends can see your activity, and so can everyone else if your profile is public

**Discover what's next**
- Trending, airing, seasonal, top rated, popular and upcoming titles, with endless scrolling
- Search with genre, format, status, release year and streaming or reading service filters
- **Describe the plot:** forgot the title? Type what you remember and Kiroku finds matching anime and manga
- A Schedule screen for upcoming episodes
- Characters, voice actors, staff and related titles
- Official reading links for manga
- Save your own reading sources for each manga, with a link and a note per site
- **Continue in Manga:** finished an anime season? Kiroku shows where to pick up the manga

**Stay up to date**
- Get a notification when a new episode of an anime you're watching comes out, even when the app is closed
- Get notified about chat messages, friend requests, recommendations and replies to your comments, and tap a notification to jump straight to it
- A Notifications screen that shows new episodes and replies in one place

**Rate and rank**
- Rate what you've completed and see the community average
- Build your own Top 10 lists for anime, manga and manhua
- Get gentle nudges about titles you've wishlisted, paused or dropped
- Rate Kiroku itself and see what other users think

**Share with friends**
- Public or private profiles
- Find people from suggestions, the most active users this week, or search, then add friends and see who's online
- **Taste match:** open a friend or any public profile to see how alike your tastes are, the titles you both loved and where you disagree
- Chat with friends and send them recommendations
- Comments on every title, with replies and notifications when someone answers you
- Vote in the **Weekly poll** from Home or Community and see how everyone else voted
- Climb the global and friends leaderboards

**Smooth and reliable**
- Fast loading with cached data and skeleton screens instead of blank pages
- Clear offline and AniList-outage messages, with a Retry button
- Bottom sheets that slide in smoothly and can be swiped down to close

**Easy to start**
- Sign in with email, Google, AniList or MyAnimeList
- Import your existing list from AniList or MyAnimeList
- Sync your library any time to pull in your latest AniList and MyAnimeList updates

## What Kiroku isn't

Kiroku is for tracking and discovery only. It has no built-in reader and no streaming.
